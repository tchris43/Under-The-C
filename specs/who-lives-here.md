# Who Lives Here? (site/who-lives-here/)

Levels 1 and 2. Tests: `tests/e2e/test_who_lives_here.py`.

1. A game is ten rounds. Each round earns a sticker of its friend(s), with a gold star if it was right first try.
2. The shared setup screen comes first (see kit.md): friends and level. Only the chosen friends come up.
   During play there are no settings buttons on screen, only the settings icon, which brings the setup screen back.

## Level 1
3. One plain note on the grand staff, placed left to right by pitch.
4. Three friend cards to pick from, all different, one right.
5. Right pick: the friend flies onto the note, its home lights up, sparkles, its note plays.
6. Wrong pick: that card greys out, that friend's own note plays, and it appears faintly where it really lives. "Try again!"

## Level 2
7. Two notes, a step or a skip apart. Name the first friend, then the second.
8. Then answer Step (next-door neighbours) or Skip (jump over one). The Step picture goes E to F; the Skip picture goes E to G.
9. Right answer: an arc joins the two; a skip also shows the friend jumped over.
10. Wrong answer: a gentle hint; for a skip, the friend in between appears.

11. Laptop: 1, 2, 3 pick a card; H plays the notes. No spoken voice.
