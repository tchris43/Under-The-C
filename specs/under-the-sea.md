# Under the Sea (site/under-the-sea/)

Meet the friends. Tests: `tests/e2e/test_under_the_sea.py`.

1. Opens on Under the Sea: the seven sea friends plus Charlie Cat, with matching piano keys.
2. Tapping a friend plays its note, makes it hop, highlights its line or space, and shows its card: letter, name, where it lives, story.
3. Tapping a piano key does the same for that key's friend. Sliding across keys plays each one.
4. Right hand shows the five land friends; Everyone shows all twelve. The picture zooms to that part of the staff.
5. Coco lives inside his circus tent in his space; Daisy has her hoop on the middle line; Finn lies between the bass clef dots.
6. Parade plays every friend on screen from low to high, then cheers.
7. Laptop: the piano rows play the keys (shown on the keys); Note letters mode types A–G; arrows walk up and down.
8. No spoken voice. Only piano sounds.
