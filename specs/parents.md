# For parents (site/parents/)

A plain page that tells a parent what the games are and lets them try one. Tests: `tests/e2e/test_parents.py`,
plus every site-wide promise in kit.md (no errors, no outside requests, security rules, fits a phone, big buttons,
works offline).

1. Headline: "The way a real piano teacher teaches notes: one friend at a time."
2. One line under it: made by a piano teacher for her students.
3. A picture of the games (`shot.png`, a placeholder until Taylor adds a real one).
4. A "Try it free" button that opens Who Lives Here?.
5. Coming soon: "Story Mode. Your child unlocks a new Note Friend for every note they master. About $4.99 one-time.
   Want it?" with a "Get the early link" button. It opens Taylor's sign-up form on Google Forms in a new tab, and
   passes nothing to it: no referrer, no link back to our page.
6. The same note about what is counted as the home page, word for word.
7. No forms or text boxes on our site: a parent never types anything here.
